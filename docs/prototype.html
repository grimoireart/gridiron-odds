How it works

Every team starts at 1500. After each game the winner takes points from the loser; the amount depends on how surprising the result was and on the margin of victory, discounted when a heavy favorite runs up the score. The gap between two ratings converts to a win probability with the standard Elo curve: a 100-point edge is roughly 64%, 200 points roughly 76%.

What's in the model

Home field ( Elo points, zero at neutral sites), rest-day difference ( points per day), margin-of-victory multiplier, and a preseason reset that pulls every team of the way back toward average. Playoff odds

The remaining schedule is simulated 10,000 times using these probabilities. Division winners and wild cards are seeded by record with Elo as a stand-in for the NFL's actual tiebreakers, then a bracket is played out with home field to the higher seed and a neutral Super Bowl.
